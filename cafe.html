Our Story

Roasted local. Baked fresh. Served with heart.

Windsor Urban Café was built on one simple idea: great coffee shouldn't be complicated. We partner with growers who care about their craft, roast in small batches, and bake every pastry from scratch each morning using fresh, local ingredients whenever we can.

Whether you're cramming for finals, grabbing a quick catch-up, or bringing the whole family in for a weekend treat, we've built this space for you — warm, unpretentious, and always buzzing with good energy.

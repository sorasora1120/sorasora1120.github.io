FAQ

Questions

QI don't know what I need yet. Can we still talk?

Of course. Tell us what you're trying to do, and we'll work out the pages and features together.

QI don't have photos or text ready.

We'll write a first draft of the text for you. For photos, we can use your phone photos or suggest stock photos that fit.

QCan I ask for changes?

Yes. You review the design and the finished site before launch. We'll tell you how many rounds of changes are included when we quote.

QHow long does it take?

A one-page site takes about a week. Bigger sites depend on the pages and features, and we'll give you a schedule with the quote.

QHow do I pay?

Half to start and half when your site is live.

QCan I update the site myself?

If we build it with WordPress or STUDIO, yes. If you'd rather not, our care plan from $25 a month covers it.

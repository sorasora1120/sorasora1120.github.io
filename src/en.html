FAQ

Questions

QIs the preview really free?

Yes. We make it, you look at it on your phone. If you don't like it, that's the end of it.

Phone photos are fine, and we'll write a first draft of the text for you.

QHow long does it take?

About a week for a one-page site, after we get your menu and photos.

QHow do I pay?

Half to start and half when your site is live.

QAre there monthly fees?

No hosting fees. Your web address is about $20 a year. Updates are optional, $25 a month or $20 per change.

QCan I change things later?

Yes. Send us a message with the new menu, prices or photos and we'll update it.
